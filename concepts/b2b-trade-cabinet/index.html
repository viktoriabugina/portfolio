    What this screen is designed to prove

    1. One status model for every deal. RFQ, offer, contract, payment and shipment share the same chips, colours and “next action”, always with a text label, never colour alone. The top ledger only shows what is waiting on this user.
    2. Compare by what it really costs. Open the polyester yarn request: the cheapest quote is EXW, so after freight it isn’t the best deal. The platform shows landed cost and says so.
    3. Four languages from day one. Switch EN / RU / UZ in the top bar: the same layout holds the longest Russian and Uzbek strings without truncating key data. Dates and money are formatted per locale; switch USD / UZS too.
    4. Contract approval as a visible path. Open the spare parts request to sign: each step shows who acts next, and signing moves the deal on everywhere at once.
    5. Ready for Vue/Nuxt. Every element here maps to a reusable component with states (empty, hover, focus, disabled, done) and tokens for colour, spacing and type.

    Concept by Viktoria V. for the NEWMIND B2B platform brief. Company names, prices, exchange rate and documents are sample data. “Karvon” is a working title only.
