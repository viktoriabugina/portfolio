About us

A new company with an old-school work ethic.

Ironbark was started by builders who spent years on other people's sites and knew they could run a better one. We keep the team small so the person who quotes your job is the person who manages it.

Every project gets a written scope, a schedule you can hold us to and a single point of contact. We'd rather tell you a hard truth at the quote stage than surprise you halfway through the build.

How we work

From first call to handover

  1. Site visit

    We walk the site with you and talk through budget, timing and must-haves.

  2. Fixed quote

    A written, itemised quote with inclusions and exclusions spelled out.

  3. Approvals

    We help with plans, engineering and council or certifier approvals.

  4. Build

    One site manager, weekly photo updates and a schedule you can see.

  5. Handover

    Final walkthrough, defects list closed out and warranty documents in hand.
