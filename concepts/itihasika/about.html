Then one day

They come home with questions that stop you in your tracks. Questions their friends ask, their teachers raise, and you are suddenly expected to answer.

SYour sonAfter school

“Dad, my friends laugh at me… is Hinduism all about caste and holy cows?”

DYour daughterAt the dinner table

“Mom, my teacher taught that Hindus have hundreds of Gods. My friends think we worship demons.”

You realize: you’ve taught them how to get ahead, but not how to stand tall in who they are.

Where we come in

That’s where Itihasika steps in.

Books, videos and courses for ages 3 to 13, built on research-backed methods.

We help you raise proud, rooted Hindu kids who understand their history, values, and identity, and can answer tough questions with confidence.

Browse our booksEnroll in our courses

  1. Crawl: build a strong foundation

    Kids first learn the heroes, festivals and ideas of their heritage, one letter and one story at a time.

  2. Walk: reinforce it together

    Games and family activities turn what they learned into something they talk about at the dinner table.

  3. Run: dive deeper

    Older kids move on to the epics, the Gita and the long story of Indian civilization.

  4. For life: identity and pride

    Each stage builds on the last, while creating joyful, memorable family experiences.

Give your child the gift of identity, confidence, and pride in their culture, for life.
