हमारी कहानी Our story

You’ve done everything to prepare your child for success.

  • Good schools.
  • Great grades.
  • STEM skills.
  • Confidence.
प्रश्न

Then one day they come home with questions that stop you in your tracks:

Your son“Dad, my friends laugh at me… is Hinduism all about caste and holy cows?”
Your daughter“Mom, my teacher taught that Hindus have hundreds of Gods. My friends think we worship demons.”

You realize: you’ve taught them how to get ahead, but not how to stand tall in who they are.

अध्याय १ Where we come in

That’s where Itihasika steps in.

We help you raise proud, rooted Hindu kids who understand their history, values, and identity — and can answer tough questions with confidence.

Our books, videos, and courses use research-backed methods, child psychology, and gamification to make learning not just easy but exciting.

Our approach

Crawl, walk, run.

Each stage builds on the last, while creating joyful, memorable family experiences.

  1. १
    Crawl

    Build a strong foundation

    Kids first learn the heroes, festivals and ideas of their heritage, one letter and one story at a time.

  2. २
    Walk

    Reinforce it together

    Games and family activities turn what they learned into something they talk about at the dinner table.

  3. ३
    Run

    Dive deeper

    Older kids move on to advanced concepts: the epics, the Gita and the long story of Indian civilization.

Give your child the gift of identity, confidence, and pride in their culture — for life.
