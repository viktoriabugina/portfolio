Questions

Good to know.

What is Itihasika TV?

Our streaming channel with animated films about India’s history, made for children and the whole family. The films go era by era, from the Indus valley civilization to modern India.

Where can I watch?

On your phone, tablet, computer and smart TV. Sign in with the same account on any device.

Will I be charged automatically?

Yes, your plan renews at the end of each month or year until you cancel. We’ll email you before a yearly renewal.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel in a couple of clicks from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Which age are the films for?

They’re made for kids aged 3 to 13, and parents enjoy them too. Every film is family-friendly.

How does a gift membership work?

Choose Gift a year, add the recipient’s email and a message, and pick the date. They get a code to start watching, and it doesn’t renew automatically.

Ready to travel through time?

Start with the Yearly plan and get two months free, or try a month first.
