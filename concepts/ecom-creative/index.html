Two concept campaigns for invented premium brands, built to show how one idea stays consistent from the website hero to ads, email, product pages and Amazon listings, with desktop and mobile versions of each.

Ember & Comb. “Buckwheat is back.”

DTC food brandSeasonal launchWeb · Meta · Google · Email

The idea: a limited autumn harvest of dark buckwheat honey. One visual system, a honeycomb texture, a slow drip and the jar as the hero, adapted for every placement so the customer recognises the campaign instantly, whether they meet it in a feed, an inbox or on the homepage.

Variations for testing: each placement has one product-led and one proof-led version, so the team can A/B test the message, not just the colours. Headlines are live text in the Figma source, so new offers drop in without redrawing.

Haloe. Making “how to use it” look beautiful.

Beauty brandShopify product pageAmazon listing imagesHow-to & guides

The idea: an education-first skincare brand. Product facts become stylish guides instead of a manual. On Shopify the text stays live over the image; for Amazon the text is built into the image to Amazon’s requirements, with a pure white main image.

About these concepts: Ember & Comb and Haloe are brands I invented to show my process; the reviews and numbers are sample content. Everything on this page is drawn as vector and live text, so each piece can be resized for new placements and edited in Figma or the Shopify theme editor.
