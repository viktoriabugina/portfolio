The customer journey today

How skincare brands lose customers

The sale is not lost at checkout. It is lost one screen earlier — when an uncertain customer leaves your website to ask somewhere else and never comes back.

  1. Visits your website

    They are unsure about an ingredient, their skin, or which product is right.

  2. Takes a screenshot

    They capture the product page or ingredient list instead of asking your brand.

  3. Asks external AI

    “Is this suitable for my skin?” The answer happens outside your website.

  4. Gets a generic choice

    The assistant suggests what appears easiest or most convenient to buy.

  5. Redirected and lost

    They move to Amazon or a rival, taking the sale and relationship with them.

  • Sale lost
  • Customer data lost
  • Relationship lost
