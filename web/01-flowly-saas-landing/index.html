FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to move my tasks from another tool?

No. Flowly works on top of the tools you already use and can sync with Jira, Asana and Trello.

How does the AI decide who owns a task?

It uses the routing rules you write in plain English, plus who handled similar requests before. You can always reassign.

Is my data used to train AI models?

Never. Your workspace data stays private and is only used to run your own automations.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — monthly plans can be cancelled in one click, and yearly plans are refunded pro rata.
