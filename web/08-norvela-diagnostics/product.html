FAQ

Questions clinics ask us

How long does installation take?

Usually one visit. An engineer places and calibrates the device, connects it to your network and runs a test panel with your staff.

Do we need a laboratory specialist to run it?

No. The on-screen workflow guides nurses and assistants through each step, and we train every shift during installation.

Can results go into our clinic software?

Yes. Core 2 exports results in common formats, and our team helps connect it to your system during setup.

What happens if the device needs repair?

We send a replacement device while yours is serviced, so testing doesn’t stop.
