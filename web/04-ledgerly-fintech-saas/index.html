FAQ

Questions finance leads ask us

Does Ledgerly replace my accountant?

No. It removes the data entry so your accountant spends their hours on tax, advice and sign-off instead of matching receipts.

Which banks are supported?

More than 40 banks across the EU, UK and US through open-banking connections, plus Stripe, Wise, Revolut Business and Paddle.

How long does setup take?

Connecting accounts takes about five minutes. Most teams run their first assisted close within a week.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period and you keep a full export of your ledger.
